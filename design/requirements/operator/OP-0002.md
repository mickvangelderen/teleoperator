# OP-0002: Fresh tracking takes priority

**Tags**: protocol, transport

## Description

Tracking should prioritize fresh input and low latency.
New samples must be usable without waiting for missing messages, loss recovery, or acknowledgements.
Control should be reliable and ordered.
Tracking should be unordered with zero retries and no decoding dependencies on earlier tracking packets.

## Verification

Information travelling over the protocol should be classified as either:

- latency critical
- reliability critical

The latency critical information should be sent over an unordered channel without retransmission.
Reliability critical information should be sent over an ordered guaranteed delivery channel.
