# PRO-0022: Prediction horizons

**Tags**: protocol

## Description

A prediction horizon is the interval between from a sample group's acquisition time to the requested time represented by those samples ([PRO-0012](./PRO-0012.md)).

The representation time of samples can not be assumed to equal the acquisition time plus the prediction horizon. 

## Rationale

Tracking APIs do not necessarily guarantee that returned samples represent the requested prediction time.
