# PRO-0022: Prediction horizons

**Tags**: protocol

## Description

`DeliverySchedulePeriodic` specifies the desired prediction horizon for samples that support prediction ([PRO-0026](./PRO-0026.md#deliveryscheduleperiodic)).
The horizon is the interval from the group's acquisition time to the requested time represented by those samples ([PRO-0012](./PRO-0012.md)).

Devices may deviate from a requested horizon, including to share acquisitions and prediction work across subscriptions or clients.

## Rationale

Tracking APIs do not necessarily guarantee that returned samples represent the requested prediction time.
